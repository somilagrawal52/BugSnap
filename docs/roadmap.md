# After the core beta

Launch gates are listed in `security.md` and `verification.md`. The following are deliberately deferred:

- Payments: checkout, billing webhooks, plan changes, renewal/cancellation and billing portal.
- Operator UX for workspace deletion, ownership transfer, retention/export and account erasure.
- Background export reconciliation at scale and richer monitoring.
- Strict nonce CSP, browser compatibility matrix and broader accessibility testing.
- AI diagnosis/fixes/duplicate detection, recording, network/console capture, browser extension.
- Jira, Linear, Slack, advanced workflows and a general-purpose authenticated public API.
- Enterprise SSO, complex hierarchies, custom domains, white-labeling, dark mode and native mobile apps.

No roadmap item is exposed as a working purchase or successful integration.
